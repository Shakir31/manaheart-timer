Manaheart logo

Manaheart - Logo.pdf      The three presentation boards: symbol and sizes, lockups and colour, app icon.
presentation/             The same boards as PNGs (2x).

svg/   Vector masters with the wordmark outlined, so no font is needed.
  manaheart-symbol-<colour>-<size>.svg
      colour: dark (for dark grounds), light (for light grounds), mono-dark (pale, one colour), mono-light (ink, one colour)
      size:   micro (16-24px), small (32-48px), full (64px and up). Use the smallest version that is at least the display size.
  manaheart-horizontal-<colour>.svg, manaheart-stacked-<colour>.svg   Symbol plus wordmark.
  manaheart-app-icon.svg           Full-bleed square master for app stores (they apply their own corner mask).
  manaheart-app-icon-rounded.svg   Rounded version for previews and mockups.

png/   Transparent PNGs. Symbols at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and 512px in every colour;
       lockups at 1600px (horizontal) and 800px (stacked) wide; app icon at 1024, 512, 192, 180 and 120px.

favicon/  favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), favicon.svg (switches to the dark colours when the browser is in dark mode),
          apple-touch-icon.png (180px).

Colours: mana #7FD4FF, pale core #CFEAFF / #F4FBFF, deep mana #4C6BFF, ink #1C2150 and #070914, pale #F1EFFA.
Wordmark: Cormorant Garamond Medium (SIL Open Font License), tracked +160 horizontal and +200 stacked.
Clear space: at least half the symbol's height on every side.
