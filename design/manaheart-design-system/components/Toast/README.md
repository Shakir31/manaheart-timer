A brief note at the bottom centre that confirms or warns without interrupting.

- `sheet` ground, 16px radius, a rim that carries the tone: `success-edge`, `edge-subtle` (neutral) or `error-edge`, plus a matching icon.
- One line of text; one optional text action (Undo, Retry). Stays 4s, longer while focused or hovered.
- `role="status"` for success and neutral, `role="alert"` for errors. Above the bottom nav on mobile.
- Not for milestones: those get their own full-screen moment.
