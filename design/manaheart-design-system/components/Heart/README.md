The mana heart: a glowing core with up to five tilted rings, and the app's only progress picture.

- Phases: gathering (faint sparks, no core) → formed (core and halo) → one to five rings → condensing (rings collapse into the core) → the next level's denser core with a slowly turning dotted ring.
- Core: `core` disc (r 9), a diamond facet in pale mana, a `mana-soft` ring at r 27, breathing (Motion). Halo: radial fade from near-white through `mana-soft` to `deep-mana`, radius grows a little with each ring.
- Rings use the geometry and states in the Motion section. Draw in a 400 × 400 viewBox; render at 300–360px on mobile, up to 640px on desktop.
- Mage paths swap only ring and core colours (`elemental-*`, `arcane-*`, `spirit-*`): Elemental adds a turbulence edge, Arcane adds square nodes and tick runes, Spirit adds a soft blurred band behind each ring.
- One SVG with `role="img"` and a label that states the circles in words. Decorative copies (behind sign-in or the focus timer) are `aria-hidden` at 20–35% opacity.
