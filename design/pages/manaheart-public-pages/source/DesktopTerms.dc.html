Terms

Last updated 8 November 2026

These terms cover using Manaheart. They're meant to be read, so they're written in plain words.

Using Manaheart

Manaheart is a personal tracker for meditation and study. It's there to support your own practice, and it isn't a medical or mental-health treatment.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself, and get in touch if you think someone else has signed in. You need to be at least 16 to create an account.

Your sessions and notes

Everything you log stays yours. We only use it to run the app for you, as the Privacy page describes.

Fair use

Please don't try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service, or use it to store anything unlawful.

Changes to the service

Manaheart will keep changing as it grows. If a feature you rely on goes away, or these terms change in a way that matters, there will be a note in the app first.

Our responsibility

Manaheart is provided as it is. We work hard to keep it running and your data safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes.

Ending things

You can stop using Manaheart and delete your account at any time. We may close an account that breaks these terms, and we'll explain why if we do.

Contact

Questions about these terms can go to hello@manaheart.example.
