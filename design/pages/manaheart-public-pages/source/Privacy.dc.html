Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Manaheart keeps very little about you, and it never sells or shares what it keeps. This page sets out exactly what that is.

On this page
  1. What we collect
  2. What we don't collect
  3. How it's used
  4. Where it's kept
  5. Exporting and deleting
  6. Changes and questions

What we collect

  • Your email address, plus your password stored as a secure hash.
  • Each session you log, meaning its type, its length, when it happened and any note you add.
  • Your settings, such as the reminder time and mage aesthetic.

What we don't collect

There are no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics and no location data. Manaheart doesn't read your contacts, calendar or anything else on your device.

How it's used

Your data is used to run the app and for nothing else. It draws your heart, fills in your sky, sends the daily reminder if it's switched on and lets you sign in on other devices.

Where it's kept

Data is stored on encrypted servers and travels over encrypted connections. A small number of providers help host the app, and they may only handle the data on our behalf.

Exporting and deleting

Every session can be downloaded as a CSV file from Settings at any time.

To delete your account and everything in it, write to privacy@manaheart.example and it will be gone within 30 days.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes in a way that matters, a note will appear in the app before the change takes effect. Questions are always welcome at privacy@manaheart.example.
