How it works

A slow ritual in three parts

  1. I

    Practice gathers into a heart

    Every session you log, whether it's a quiet sit or an hour with a textbook, adds a little mana. Once five hours have gathered, they settle into a heart that beats softly at the centre of the screen.

  2. II

    Circles form around it

    As the hours keep adding up, rings of light begin to orbit the heart, up to five of them. Each one draws itself in slowly, like a line traced in the dark.

  3. III

    Steady practice, then the next rank

    Practising most days keeps the circles stable, while long gaps leave them flickering. When all five hold still, they condense into a stronger heart and a new rank begins.

The Constellation

A night sky that fills in over time

Hours of meditation light the stars of Mind, study lights Lore, and coming back regularly lights Rhythm. Nothing announces a new star. It's just there the next time you look up.

Over the months, the shape of the sky ends up looking a lot like the shape of your practice.

What Manaheart leaves out

  • There are no streak flames, so missing a day won't put anything out.

  • Nothing is scored, ranked against strangers or traded for points.

  • The app stays quiet, apart from one daily reminder that only arrives if it's switched on.

It's a log of your practice and a heart that grows with it, and that's all it needs to be.
