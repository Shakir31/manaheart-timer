The heart

Every minute logged as meditation or study becomes mana. For the first five hours, the mana gathers as faint sparks in the middle of the screen.

At five hours it settles into a heart that beats slowly, and from then on it's there each time the app opens.

Logging a session

There are two ways to log. Begin session starts a focus timer, and the time is added when the session ends. Log manually is for practice done away from the app, where you pick meditation or study, set the length and add a note if you like.

Every session is listed under Sessions, where it can be edited or deleted.

Circles

Past the heart, the hours start drawing circles around it, up to five in all. Each new circle traces itself in a little more with every session until it closes and begins to orbit.

  • A stable circle turns cleanly and evenly.
  • An unstable circle flickers and wobbles a little.
  • A forming circle is an arc that is still being drawn.

Steady practice and missed days

Practising on most days keeps circles stable, and the Rhythm row on the home screen shows how the last seven days went.

After a long gap a circle starts to flicker again, but nothing is lost. A few steady days will settle it, and there's never a flame to go out.

Condensing and ranks

Once all five circles are stable, the heart can condense. The circles fold inward, the heart grows denser and brighter, and a new rank begins with room for five more circles.

The ranks run from Apprentice through Adept, Magus and Archmagus to Sage. Each one has a page in the Grimoire, which opens from the rank title on the home screen.

The Constellation

The third tab is a night sky that lights up over time. Meditation hours light the Mind branch, study hours light Lore, and regular practice lights Rhythm.

Bright stars are already lit, faint ones are next, and the dimmest are still a long way off. Tapping a star shows what it takes to light it.

Mage aesthetics

When the first circle forms, the heart can take on a style of its own: Elemental, Arcane or Spirit. A style only changes colours and textures, and it can be switched in Settings at any time.
