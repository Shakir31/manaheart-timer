Manaheart design system · motion

Slow, continuous, never bouncy

Ambient motion is slow enough to read as breathing, not activity. Nothing overshoots. Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation stops and meaning moves into shape, opacity and text.
Live
Spec
Reduced motion
Stable ring rotation
Rune layer travels along the ring, linear. Odd rings 40s per loop, even rings 55s reversed, so neighbours counter-turn. Ring tilt never changes.
Runes hold still. Stable still reads as one continuous, even line.
Unstable flicker & wobble
Flicker 3.4s stepped, opacity jumps between 1 and 0.3 at uneven points. Wobble ±2.5° with a 2px drift, 6.5s ease-in-out alternate. No rune travel.
Fixed at 60% opacity, broken segments only. Never flashes faster than 3 times a second in any mode.
Forming-arc growth
After a session the arc extends to its new length over 1.6s, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), led by the spark. At rest it shimmers 0.5 to 1 opacity, 2.6s alternate. Dotted ghost shows the full path.
Arc appears at its new length at once. Progress text updates; no shimmer.
Heart idle pulse
Core scale 1 to 1.06, opacity 0.88 to 1, 5s ease-in-out alternate. In a focus session the background heart slows to 6s and dims to 0.3. After a gain it quickens to 3s for one minute.
Static at full brightness. The focus timer counts in place of the pulse.
Bottom sheet
Enter: slide up from 100% over 360ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); scrim fades in over 240ms. Exit: 240ms ease-in. Drag down past 30% to dismiss.
120ms crossfade in place, no slide.
Modal & dialog
Fade plus scale 0.97 to 1 over 280ms, same curve. Exit 180ms. Toasts rise 12px and fade over 240ms, stay 4s.
120ms fade only. Toasts appear without rising.
Moments: gain, milestone, condense
Mana stream dashes travel up 2.4s loop. Headlines rise 12px over 1.2s. Condense: rings shrink to the core over 2.7s ease-in, core flares 1.5× for 0.7s, then the level-up screen fades in over 600ms.
Stream hidden. Rings fade out over 400ms, then the Level 2 heart appears static.
