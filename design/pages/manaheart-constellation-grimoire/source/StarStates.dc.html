Star states

Stars light themselves from logged practice. State is carried by size, fill and label, never colour alone: lit stars are solid with a halo, next stars are hollow and named, distant stars are bare points. Every star is a 44px tap target.

Lit
Solid core, mana halo, slow twinkle (6s). Name in secondary.
Lit since last visit
A wider, breathing halo on the next visit only. No badge, no banner.
Next
Hollow ring, name in tertiary. Joined to its lit neighbour by a dotted thread.
Distant
A bare point, no name. Fainter still in a new sky.
Rank, lit
The brightest stars: four-point spark, serif name, rising above the heart.
Rank, next
Hollow, serif italic name. Later ranks stay distant.
Selected
Dashed primary ring while its sheet is open; keyboard focus looks the same.
Threads. Fine mana line between lit neighbours; dotted thread from a lit star to the next one. Nothing joins distant stars.
Mage colour. After the first circle, halos and threads take the chosen path: Elemental, Arcane or Spirit.
