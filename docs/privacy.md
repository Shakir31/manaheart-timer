# Privacy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Manaheart is a meditation and study tracker. This page explains what the app keeps, why, and what control you have over it. It's written to be read, so it's short.

## What we keep

**Your account.** Your email address and a password. The password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored only as a secure hash, so we never see it.

**Your sessions.** For each session you log or time: whether it was meditation or study, how long it lasted, when it ended, any note you add, and whether it came from the timer or was logged by hand.

**Your settings.** Your chosen mage path, time zone, default session length, reminder preference and time, and a few markers such as whether you've seen the welcome screens.

Everything else in the app, including the heart, circles, stars and ranks, is worked out from your sessions each time it's shown and isn't stored separately.

**On your device.** The app keeps a few small things in your browser's local storage so it works smoothly: a running timer (so it survives a reload), your last chosen category, when you last opened the Constellation, and your sign-in session. These stay on your device.

## What we don't do

We don't show ads, sell your data, or share it with advertisers. We don't use third-party analytics or tracking. Your notes and sessions are only visible to you.

## Where it's stored

Your data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our database and sign-in provider, in Singapore. Access is locked down so each account can only read and change its own records.

## Your choices

- **Export.** Settings › Export data downloads every session as a CSV file.
- **Edit or delete sessions.** Any session can be changed or removed from the Sessions page.
- **Delete your account.** Settings › Delete account removes the account and all its data straight away. If you can't sign in, email [manaheart.timer@gmail.com](mailto:manaheart.timer@gmail.com) and we'll delete it within 30 days.
- **Questions or corrections.** Email [manaheart.timer@gmail.com](mailto:manaheart.timer@gmail.com).

## Children

Manaheart isn't intended for children under 13. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and mention it in the app.
