# Guide

Manaheart is a simple log for meditation and study. Practice gathers as mana and slowly builds a heart, and that's the whole idea. Here's how the pieces fit.

## Logging time

Start a focus session with **Begin session** and end it when you're done. The timer keeps counting even if the tab sleeps or the page reloads. **Switch focus** moves between meditation and study mid-session, and each part is saved separately.

Forgot to time something? **Log manually** records a session as just finished, with an optional note.

## The heart

The first five hours of practice form the heart. After that, time goes into circles that orbit it, up to five at each level.

## Rhythm and steady circles

A day counts as practised once it has at least 10 minutes in it. Practising most days builds rhythm, and one missed day in between is forgiven, so practising every other day still counts.

Rhythm steadies the circles. A circle formed through steady practice turns smoothly, while one built in bursts flickers until the rhythm catches up. Nothing is ever lost for missing days, the circles just take longer to settle.

## Condensing

Once all five circles are steady, **Condense** folds them into the heart. The heart reaches the next rank (Apprentice, Adept, Magus, Archmagus, Sage) and a new set of circles begins. Each level needs a little more time than the last.

## Mage paths

After the first circle forms, you choose a path, Elemental, Arcane or Spirit. It only changes how the heart and circles look, and it can be changed any time in Settings.

## The Constellation

The Constellation is a sky that fills in as you practise. **Mind** stars light up with meditation, **Lore** stars with study, and **Rhythm** stars with days practised and runs of days in a row. Ranks are the brightest stars above the heart. Tap any star to see what lights it.

The **Grimoire**, opened from the Constellation, keeps a page for each rank reached.

## Your data

Sessions can be edited or deleted from the Sessions page, and Settings › Export data downloads everything as a CSV file.
